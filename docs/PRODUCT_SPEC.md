Create a modern, production-ready SaaS platform called **AquaDesk** for RO water-purifier service businesses.

AquaDesk should help RO service centres manage customers, service requests, technicians, AMC plans, purifier installations, filters and spare parts, invoices, payments, reminders, reports and multiple branches from one dashboard.

The platform must be completely original in branding, design, wording, layouts and code.

Do not copy the branding, UI, content or proprietary features of any existing software.

---

# 1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW

AquaDesk is a subscription-based service management platform designed for:

* RO water-purifier service centres
* Water purifier dealers
* RO installation businesses
* Independent RO technicians
* Domestic water purifier service companies
* Commercial RO maintenance companies
* Multi-branch water treatment businesses
* Filter and spare-parts distributors

1. Customer enquiry
2. Service booking
